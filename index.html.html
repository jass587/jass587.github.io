Proptech · AI Product — Current

Senior Full Stack Engineer — Ziffy.ai (AI Property Search & Investment Platform)

Dec 2022 — Present
The problem
Real estate investors were filtering listings by rigid dropdowns instead of describing what they actually wanted — "under €500k, 7% rental yield" doesn't map to a checkbox UI.
The decision
Replaced structured filters with a natural-language search layer (LLM-parsed), then built a ranking model on top using ROI, cap rate and appreciation potential — so the product's core value became judgment, not just search.
What I owned
Sole architecture owner across frontend and FastAPI backend, the query-parsing pipeline, and the investment-ranking logic — end to end, from the ambiguous ask to the deployed feature, including the trade-off calls a lead would normally sign off on.
